About

A bit about me

Wisdom Chukwuma · full-stack developer · Lagos, Nigeria

I like building the whole thing: the database, the server, the screen people tap on, and the domain it all lives on. Then I like watching real people use it.

My biggest project is Wiscot, a social platform with real-time chat, games, a wallet and a shop, which I built alone and run on my own server. In 2026 I also built two live platforms for paying clients, one used by about 1,000 fantasy football players, and I founded StudyPath and WappyTales.

I wrote my first code in 2022, in Python, when an EduPoint tutor came to my secondary school, Radiance High School. That same year our team built a bin that opens itself when you walk up to it, with an Arduino and an ultrasonic sensor, and came second at EduPoint RoboCode Fest. I finished school in 2023 first in Coding and Robotics, with maths and physics as my strongest subjects, and started web development with HTML, CSS and JavaScript at New Horizons.
