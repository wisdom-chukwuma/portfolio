Overview

My church's Youth Week 2026 ran from 14 to 20 September, and I built the app everyone used during it.

People join with just a nickname, no sign-up, and are put into one of four squads (Crown, Throne, Altar, Sanctuary) that compete for points all week.

What I built
  • A live countdown to every session, and a check-in that only opens 30 minutes before a session starts.
  • Daily polls and reflection questions.
  • An event gallery with each day's photos and videos in a TikTok-style feed, plus a shoutout wall with likes and comments.
  • Push reminders 30 minutes before each session, sent by scheduled Cloud Functions.
