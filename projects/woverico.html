Overview

Woverico turns a topic into a finished, voiced 1080×1920 video ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Each video is an HTML motion graphic that gets recorded frame by frame and encoded to MP4.

It covers twelve formats, including breaking news, crime files, sports, story time, politics, did-you-know and hot takes, and it has produced over 110 finished videos.

What I built
  • Scenes are built as HTML and CSS animations, so layouts and branding stay consistent across every video.
  • Playwright records the animation and ffmpeg encodes it to H.264 at 1080×1920.
  • Narration with Piper (free and offline) or ElevenLabs.
  • Animated background videos for categories like sports and story time.
