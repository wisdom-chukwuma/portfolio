Overview

The Eagle Team is a distributor network for Jigsimur, a Nigerian herbal drink brand with its store in Festac Town, Lagos. They needed one place for members and admins.

Members sign in with Google, order from the store, check in at the Friday seminar, post to the team feed, climb a leaderboard and see who invited who. Admins run walk-in sales, stock, promos and attendance from an admin panel.

What I built
  • Seminar check-in only works at the store, on Fridays. It is geofenced and time-windowed, with QR and manual options for admins.
  • Distributor packs of 7 or 9 bottles draw from one shared bottle stock, so the count stays right whichever pack sells.
  • Referrals build a family tree showing who invited whom across the whole team.
  • A leaderboard, a hall of fame and a posts feed for the team.
