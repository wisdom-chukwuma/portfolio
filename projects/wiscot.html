Overview

Wiscot is the biggest thing I've built. It's a full platform on Django where people post, chat and call each other, play multiplayer games for Cots (the platform's own currency), and spend them in a shop.

I built all of it and I run it myself, on a Linux server I set up and look after. It ships on the web and on Android.

What I built
  • Real-time chat, presence and multiplayer games over WebSockets (Django Channels + Redis), with Gunicorn and Daphne behind Nginx.
  • Games you can play for practice or stake Cots on: chess, checkers, 8 ball pool, Dash and more, with a bot opponent (COTY AI) for solo play.
  • Cot Bank, a wallet with Flutterwave deposits, transfers, withdrawals, a transaction PIN and a live naira converter.
  • Cot Mining and an XP tracker that pays XP rewards into Cot Bank, plus leaderboards, Wiscot Records and account comparison.
  • A shop for templates, graphics, audio and video, and a jobs board where people apply for roles at businesses on Wiscot.
  • Voice and video calls over WebRTC through a TURN server I host (Coturn), a 3D avatar on every profile, automatic image moderation (NudeNet + OpenCV) and sign-in with Discord, GitHub, Twitter and TikTok.
